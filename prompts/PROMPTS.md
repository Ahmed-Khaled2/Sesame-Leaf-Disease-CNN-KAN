# Prompts used to generate the synthetic training images

The images are in [`synthetic_images/`](../synthetic_images). [`image_prompts.csv`](image_prompts.csv) gives the prompt
of every Gemini image (column `raw_file`), and `outputs/metadata/synthetic_v2.csv` gives the Gemini image of every
tile (columns `raw_file` and `raw_n`).

## 1. Tool, date and model

All images were generated on 9 November 2026 in the Gemini app (Google, https://gemini.google.com). The model
setting shown in the app was "Auto (Gemini 3 models)". Seven chats were used, with at most 10 prompts per chat:
P01-P10, P11-P20 and P21-P28 for Phyllody, then M01-M10, M11-M20, M21-M30 and M31-M40 for Powdery Mildew.

## 2. Procedure

1. Only the text prompts below were sent. No photo was uploaded, and no description of a dataset photo was used:
   every prompt was written from the standard description of the disease's symptoms.
2. Each prompt asks for a 2 x 2 grid of four different field photos, and sets the view of each photo, the
   background and the light.
3. P01 and P02 were a first trial. From P03 on, each of the four photos asks for a different view. No prompt was
   changed after it was sent and no follow-up was typed. The first try at M05 did not reach Gemini, so the same
   prompt was sent again.
4. Every answer was downloaded at full size. The M21 image was not saved, so there are 28 Phyllody and 39
   Powdery Mildew images.
5. `process_synthetic.py split` cut the images into 268 tiles, trimmed the white frame around each tile, and
   covered the Gemini logo with the patch just above it.
6. Every tile was checked visually, and 40 were rejected: 19 Powdery Mildew leaves with yellow and brown spots that
   look like another disease, 14 leaves held in a hand (hands were also cropped out of the real photos), 4 distant
   Phyllody shots in which the disease is hard to see, and 3 near-identical tiles of one Phyllody image.
7. `process_synthetic.py finalize` removed 6 tiles that were near-copies of another synthetic tile and drew
   75 Phyllody and 111 Powdery Mildew tiles at random (seed 2026). No tile is a near-copy of a real photo: the
   highest cosine similarity of ResNet50 features to any real photo is 0.92 for Phyllody and 0.95 for Powdery
   Mildew (the near-copy threshold is 0.97).
8. The tiles are used in training folds only (dataset `multi_syn2`), never for validation or testing.
